2000 BC
Prophet Ibrahim was thrown in fire by King Namrud in the city of Ur Mesapotamia, when he defied his claim of being GOD.

Photograph By 4028mdk09, CC BY-SA 3.0, via Wikimedia Commons

520 CE Kaaba Attacked
Ethopia King Abraha Attacked Kaaba, his forces & Elephents were crushed to Death from stones, dropped by birds Which all of sudden appeared on the sky.

Photograph By See page for author, Public domain, via Wikimedia Commons

570 CE Prophet Muhammad
Prophet Muhammad was born as an orphan in the city of Macca in the tribe Banu-Hashim, who were the custodians of Kaaba at that time

Photograph By Wikimedia via Public domain Wikimedia Commons

610 CE First Revelation
First revelation of Quran From Angel Gabriel on Mount Hira Where Prophet used to come regularly to think about GOD

Photograph By saudipics, CC BY-SA 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons

613 CE Public Preaching Starts
Prophet Muhammad begins public preaching in Mecca and was severely opposed by elites, except few poor who dared to accept Islam risking lives.

Photograph By user:Sonia Sevilla, CC0, via Wikimedia Commons

622 CE Migration To Madina
Prophet Muhammad is Invited to Madina & avails this opportunity to establish the first Islamic state in the world. Islamic Calendar starts from here.

Photograph By Fazoffic, CC0, via Wikimedia Commons

624-627 CE Key Battles
Key battles including Badr, Uhad & the Battle of the Trench are fought between Muslims and non-beleivers. victories in battles consolidated Islam.

Photograph By Adiput Public domain, via Wikimedia Commons

630 CE Mecca Conquered
Mecca is conquered; Islam is fully established in the Arabian Peninsula, idols were removed from Kaaba, Haj was performed first time in the Islamic way.

Photograph By Muhammad Sadiq Bey via Wikimedia Commons CC BY-SA 3.0

632 CE Prophet leaves world.
Prophet Muhammad passes away, Abu Bakr becomes the first Caliph, initiating the Rashidun Caliphate which lasts for the next 30 years.

Photograph By بلال الدويك, CC BY-SA 3.0, via Wikimedia Commons

632-661 CE Khilafate-Rashdeen
Next 30 years see expansion of Islam into the neighbouring powerful Byzantine & Sassanid territories and the first Muslim civil war.

Photograph By Wario2 CC0, via Wikimedia Commons

632 CE Abu Bakr
Prophet's ally Abu Bakr takes over. After being elected as the first Khalifa, immediately dispatched prophet's incompleted expedition to Syrian border.

Photograph By Mark Ahsmann, CC BY-SA 3.0, via Wikimedia Commons

633 CE Rebellions
Rebellions break out across Arabia as several tribes refused to pay Zakat or followed false prophets. forces were dispatched to control the rebellions.

Photograph By Mohammad adil at the English-language Wikipedia, CC BY-SA 3.0, Wikimedia Commons

633 CE First Compilation of Quran
Following high casualties of memorizers, Zyad ibn Thabit was ordered to collect and compile Quran into a single and authentic volume.

Photograph By AnonymousUnknown author, Public domain, via Wikimedia Commons

633-634 War with neighbouring powers.
Prophet's earlier coordinated military campaigns were launched into the borders of Persian & Byzantine Empires.

Photograph By Heretiq, CC BY-SA 3.0, via Wikimedia Commons

634 CE Abu Bakr Leaves World
First Khalifa Passes away after nominating Umar ibn al-Khattab to succeed him as the next leader of the newly formed Islamic state.

Photograph By i_pinz from Vienna, AT, CC BY-SA 2.0, via Wikimedia Commons

634 CE The Second Khalifa
Umar bin Khattab takes on. His caliphate period lasts for 10 years with unprecedent victories over great empires of Byzantine and Sassanid

Photograph By Bassem, CC BY-SA 3.0, via Wikimedia Commons

634 CE Battle of Ajnadayn
A decisive clash resulted in Rashedeen victory over Byzantine forces in Palestine opening the door of Conquests in Levant.

Photograph By Yoav Dothan, GFDL, via Wikimedia Commons

636 CE Battle of Yarmouk
Monumental six-day war where Muslim forces defeat the Byzantine army under tactical command of Khālid ibn al-Walīd.

Photograph By Mohammad adil at the English-language Wikipedia, CC BY-SA 3.0, Wikimedia Commons

636 CE Battle of al-Qadisiyyah
Led by Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas, the Muslim forces decisively defeat Sasanians, thus cracking Persian Sasani control over Iraq.

Photograph By Mohammad adil at the English-language Wikipedia, CC BY-SA 3.0, via Wikimedia Commons

639-642 CE Conquest of Egypt
Amr ibn al-Aas leads the Egypt campaign, captures historical Babylon & Alexandria, ending Byzantine & starting Muslim rule.

Photograph By Mohammad Adilderivative work: Freedom's Falcon, CC BY-SA 3.0, via Wikimedia Commons

642 CE Battle of Nahavand
Victory of Victories, won by Muslim army under Al-Numan ibn Muqarrin. This war gave Sasanians a catastrophic defeat.

Photograph By Eugène Flandin, Public domain, via Wikimedia Commons

644 CE Second Khalifa Assassinated.
Mortally wounded while leading prayer. Before passing away appointed a six-man committee to choose the next Caliph.

Photograph By Mohammad adil at English Wikipedia, CC BY-SA 3.0, via Wikimedia Commons

644 CE Uthman ibn Affan elected Third Caliph
His caliphate lasted 12 years First phase, major expansion & prosperity and then phase of rising internal dissent

Photograph By Vikiçizer, CC BY-SA 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons

647-649 CE Formation of First Muslim Navy
Authorizes Muawiyah(governor of Syria) to build the first Islamic naval fleet leading to the conquest of Cyprus.

Photograph By Cattette, CC BY 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons

651-654 CE Expansion continue east & west
Military expeditions are pushed deeper into North Africa, coastal parts of Europe and across Central Asia.

Photograph By Jean-Pierre Dalbéra from Paris, France, CC BY 2.0, via Wikimedia Commons

656 CE Assassination of Third Caliph
Political factions, regional discontents result in besiege of Uthman's house & his assassination while reading the Quran.

Photograph By Topkapı Palace Museum Library, Public domain, via Wikimedia Commons

656 CE Ascension of The Fourth Khalifa
Ali ibn Abi Talib elected as fourth Caliph amidst heavy political turmoil in Yathrub. He Shifted capital from Yathrub to Kufa,Iraq.

Photograph By Bjelica, CC BY-SA 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons

657 CE Battle of the Siffin
Battle between Fourth Khalifa and Muawiyah (governor of Syria) ended in acceptance by both sides of a political arbitration.

Photograph By The original uploader was Zeledi at English Wikipedia., CC BY-SA 3.0, via Wikimedia Commons

658 Battle of Nahrawan
A radical faction(Kharijites) breaks away from Khalifa after rejecting arbitration announced during the battle of siffin.

Photograph By Gertrude Bell (verstorben 1926), Public domain, via Wikimedia Commons

661 CE End of Rashedeen Chaliphate
Ali Ibn Talib Assassiniated in Kufa by a Kharijite al-Rahman ibn Muljam. Hassan ibn Ali, succeeds but abdicates to avoid further bloodshed.

Photograph By See page for author, Public domain, via Wikimedia Commons
