Islamic Chronology
Important Dates of the Islamic
expansion, rapid military advances,
dynistic rules, power struggle,
cultural advances and civil wars.
2000 BC
Prophet Ibrahim, was put on fire
by King Namrud in the city of Ur
Mesapotamia, when he defied
his claim of being GOD
520 CE
Ethiopian King Abraha Attacked Mecca.
His forces & Elephents were crushed
to Death from stones, dropped by birds
Which all of sudden appeared on the sky
570 CE
Prophet Muhammad was born
as an orphan in the city of Mecca
in the tribe Banu-Hashim, who were
the custodians of Kaaba at that time
610 CE
First revelation received from
Angel Gabriel on Mount Hira
Where Prophet used to come regularly
to think about GOD
613 CE
Prophet Muhammad begins public
preaching in Mecca and was severely
opposed by city elites, except few poor
who dared to accept Islam risking lives.
622 CE
Prophet Muhammad receives invitation
from Madina and avails this opportunity
to establish the first Islamic state in the
world. Islamic Calendar starts from here.
624-627 CE
Key battles including Badr, Uhad and
the Battle of the Trench are fought
between Muslims and non-beleivers.
victories in battles consolidated Islam.
630 CE
Mecca is conquered; Islam gets fully
established in the Arabian Peninsula
Idols are removed from Kaaba. Haj is
performed first time in the Islamic way.
632 CE
Prophet Muhammad passes away,
Abu Bakr becomes the first Caliph
initiating the Rashidun Caliphate
which lasts for the next 30 years
632-661 CE
Khilafate-Rashdeen (four rightly guided)
starts and lasts for the next 30 years,
with expansion into Byzantine & Sassanid
territories and first civil war in Muslims.
